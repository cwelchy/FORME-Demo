    An agent with a memory

    Every workout and every text makes the next suggestion smarter.

    The agent keeps each member's training history, purchases and past conversations in context. It reasons about their goals, what they already own and what they asked for, then puts the most relevant program, coach or upgrade in front of them at the right moment. Pick a member to see it work.
